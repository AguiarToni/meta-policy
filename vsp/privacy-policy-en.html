Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

App: Video Suite Pro

Application: VSP – Video Suite Pro (tatdigital.com/video-studio-pro)

Operated by: TATDigital

This policy covers the social connection and publishing features of Video Suite Pro: Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, and Google/YouTube. Sections 1–7 describe Meta; sections 8–10 add TikTok, YouTube, and shared safeguards. Integration availability depends on the features enabled for your account.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Facebook or Instagram account to VSP, we access and store the following data through the Meta Graph API:

  • Your Facebook user ID and name
  • Your Facebook Page name, Page ID, and Page access token
  • Your connected Instagram professional account ID and username
  • OAuth access tokens and expiration information required to publish content on your behalf

We associate the connection with your VSP account. Publishing records include the video reference, selected destination, title and caption, publication settings, dates, processing status, and identifiers or links returned by Meta.

This integration does not request access to your personal messages, contacts, or friends list.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Publish videos and captions as Reels to your connected Instagram professional account
  • Publish videos and captions to your Facebook Page
  • Display your available destinations and connection status within VSP
  • Check publication status and recover the result of an existing submission without duplicating it

VSP submits content only after you review and confirm the publication. Connecting an account or creating a video does not by itself authorize publication.

We do not use data obtained through this integration for advertising, profiling, or purposes other than those listed above.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in our database and are not exposed to the browser. Communication with Meta's Graph API occurs server-side over HTTPS.

To publish your video, VSP sends Meta the selected content and publication settings, including a URL from which Meta can retrieve the video from VSP's media storage. You authorize access on Meta; VSP does not ask for your Facebook or Instagram password.

4. Data Retention

Connection data is retained while the connection remains active and is needed to provide the service. Publication records are retained to display and recover submission results until you request their deletion.

Disconnecting removes the stored connection after successful revocation. It does not automatically delete all publication records. You may request deletion of the associated social data by email as described below.

5. Third-Party Services

VSP uses the Meta Graph API (Facebook / Instagram) to publish content. Your use of these services is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

Deleting data from VSP does not remove videos already published on Facebook or Instagram. You can remove those publications directly on the relevant platform.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can withdraw the connection or request deletion of the social data associated with your VSP account:

  • Use "Desconectar" in "Conectar redes sociales" in VSP to revoke the connection. Facebook and Instagram share the VSP Meta authorization, so disconnecting it can disconnect both networks in VSP.
  • For deletion of connection data and publication records, email tatdigital@tatdigital.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request – VSP" and the email address associated with your VSP account.

We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

See the User Data Deletion Instructions for the procedure and scope. Deleting social connection data does not automatically delete your original VSP projects or videos.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions: tatdigital@tatdigital.com

8. TikTok connection and publication

VSP requests user.info.basic to identify your connected TikTok account and video.publish to submit videos you confirm. It stores your TikTok open_id, display name, encrypted access and refresh tokens, granted scopes, expiry information, and connection dates against your VSP account.

Before publication, VSP queries TikTok creator information, including the account nickname, username, avatar URL, available privacy settings, and limits on duration, comments, Duet, and Stitch. This information is used to display the destination and applicable publishing options.

When you confirm a publication, VSP sends TikTok the selected video's media URL, caption, visibility and interaction choices, and applicable commercial-content and synthetic-content declarations. Publication records include the selected video and destination, these choices, music and branded-content confirmations, dates, processing status, and publication identifiers or links returned by TikTok. VSP uses these records to track and recover the submission result.

TikTok connection data is retained while needed for the authorized connection; publication records remain until deletion is requested. You can disconnect TikTok in VSP or withdraw access in TikTok's app-permissions settings. Successful disconnection removes the stored connection and tokens; it does not automatically remove the publication history. Request full deletion by emailing tatdigital@tatdigital.com with your VSP account email and the affected TikTok account. We acknowledge requests within 48 hours and process deletion without undue delay, within a maximum of 30 days unless a shorter applicable deadline is required. This is VSP's service commitment, not a claimed universal 30-day TikTok rule.

TikTok processing is subject to its Privacy Policy. TikTok's Developer Data Sharing Agreement, sections 3.1(e) and 5.3, requires cooperation with data-subject rights and compliance with applicable data-protection law. Its Developer Terms, section VI, require deletion of TikTok information on termination. Deleting VSP data does not delete videos already posted to TikTok.

9. Google/YouTube connection and publication

VSP uses YouTube API Services. It requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload to upload the videos you confirm and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly to identify your channel and read the processing status of videos submitted through VSP. This integration does not request Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Contacts access.

VSP reads channel information and stores your channel ID and name, encrypted access and refresh tokens, granted scopes, expiry information, and connection dates against your VSP account. When you confirm an upload, VSP sends YouTube the video file, title, description, visibility, made-for-kids choice, and synthetic-content declaration. Publication records contain the video reference, destination, submitted options, dates, upload and processing status, and video identifiers or links returned by YouTube.

This data is used to connect your channel, perform uploads you authorize, show their status, and recover existing submissions. Google/YouTube data obtained through this integration is not used for advertising, profiling, or training generative AI models, and is not sold.

You can revoke access in VSP or at Google's security permissions page. For deletion requests or privacy questions, email tatdigital@tatdigital.com, specifying your VSP account and YouTube channel. Requests concerning stored YouTube user data are handled as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days; revocation through VSP also requires deletion of the associated authorized data within 7 calendar days. Revocation through Google's security page requires deletion as soon as possible and within 30 calendar days. Deleting your VSP account also requires deletion of associated YouTube user data within 7 calendar days. See YouTube Developer Policies, III.D.3 and III.E.4.

YouTube API metadata is subject to refresh or deletion within 30 calendar days under III.E.4 of those policies; authorization tokens may be retained only while necessary for the active authorization. The Meta retention period in section 4 does not override these YouTube requirements. The current VSP disconnect control revokes authorization and removes connection credentials; it does not automatically purge every publication record. Full-record deletion requests use the contact procedure above.

Deleting data held by VSP does not delete your YouTube channel or uploaded videos; manage those directly in YouTube. Your use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Shared safeguards and scope

Social authorization tokens are encrypted in the VSP database and used by the server over HTTPS. VSP does not ask for your social-network passwords. The authorization flow uses a secure, HTTP-only browser cookie that expires after 10 minutes to bind the authorization response to the browser that started it.

Social data is associated with your VSP account and used for the connection and publication functions described here. The selected media and publishing options are shared with the platform you choose; the VSP hosting and media-storage services process the data needed to operate those functions. Original VSP projects and video files are distinct from social API records and are not automatically removed by disconnecting a social account.

See the data-deletion instructions and VSP Terms of Service. General VSP account and website processing is also described in the TATDigital privacy policy.
