User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 11, 2026

App: Video Suite Pro

Application: VSP – Video Suite Pro (tatdigital.com/video-studio-pro)

Operated by: TATDigital

These instructions cover Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, and Google/YouTube. The first two options below retain the Meta procedure; the additional sections describe TikTok and YouTube.

How to Delete Your Data

If you have connected your Facebook or Instagram account to VSP, you can disconnect the authorization from within the application. To delete all associated social data, including publication records, submit an email request.

Option 1 – Disconnect from within the app

  1. Log in to VSP at tatdigital.com/video-studio-pro
  2. Go to "Conectar redes sociales"
  3. Click "Desconectar" for the connected account and confirm the action
  4. Check that the application confirms the disconnection. After successful revocation, the stored connection and its tokens are removed.

Facebook and Instagram share the VSP Meta authorization: disconnecting it can disconnect both networks within VSP. Disconnecting does not automatically delete the full publication history; use the email procedure below to request that deletion.

Option 2 – Email request for deletion of associated social data

  1. Send an email to tatdigital@tatdigital.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request – VSP
  3. Include the email address associated with your VSP account and indicate the connected Facebook or Instagram accounts concerned

We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days. A confirmation email will be sent once the process is complete. We may request confirmation of account ownership before deleting data. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

What Data Is Deleted

A request to delete all associated Meta social data covers:

  • Stored Facebook and Instagram access tokens
  • Stored Facebook user ID and name associated with the connection
  • Facebook Page IDs and Page names
  • Connected Instagram professional account IDs and usernames
  • Associated publication records, including video references, captions, destinations, settings, status, and publication identifiers

This request does not automatically delete your original VSP projects or video files. If you also want to delete other VSP account data, state that in your request so its scope can be confirmed.

Videos already published on Facebook or Instagram remain on those platforms until you delete them there.

TikTok data deletion

In VSP, open "Conectar redes sociales" and choose "Desconectar" for TikTok. After TikTok confirms revocation, VSP removes the connection record and its stored access and refresh tokens. This action does not automatically delete the publication history. You can also withdraw VSP's access in TikTok's app-permissions settings.

For full deletion, email tatdigital@tatdigital.com with subject "Data Deletion Request – VSP", your VSP account email, and the affected TikTok account. The request covers stored open_id and display name, tokens, scopes and connection information, and associated publication records: video references, captions, destinations, publishing choices and confirmations, dates, status, and returned publication identifiers or links.

We acknowledge the request within 48 hours and process deletion without undue delay, within a maximum of 30 days unless a shorter applicable deadline is required. This is VSP's service commitment. TikTok's Developer Data Sharing Agreement, sections 3.1(e) and 5.3, requires cooperation with data-subject rights and compliance with applicable data-protection law, rather than setting this 30-day service commitment. Its Developer Terms, section VI, require deletion of TikTok information on termination.

Google/YouTube data deletion and revocation

Use "Desconectar" for YouTube in VSP, or revoke VSP's access at Google's security permissions page. The VSP control requests token revocation and removes connection credentials after successful revocation; it does not automatically purge every publication record.

To request deletion of stored YouTube user data, email tatdigital@tatdigital.com with subject "Data Deletion Request – VSP", your VSP account email, and your YouTube channel. This covers stored channel ID and name, access and refresh tokens, scopes and connection information, and associated publication records, including video references, submitted metadata and settings, upload state, processing status, dates, and returned video identifiers or links. Do not send passwords or tokens.

We acknowledge email requests within 48 hours. YouTube data-deletion requests are handled as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days. Revoking consent through VSP or deleting your VSP account also requires deletion of associated YouTube user data within 7 calendar days. Revocation through Google's security page requires deletion as soon as possible and within 30 calendar days. The 30-day Meta email-deletion commitment above does not extend the 7-day YouTube deadline. Source: YouTube Developer Policies, III.D.3 and III.E.4.

These requirements apply to all relevant stored YouTube data, including publication records; removal of tokens alone does not complete a full-data deletion. The contact procedure handles full-record deletion requests, and confirmation is sent when the requested deletion is complete.

Scope and deadlines

The 48-hour acknowledgement and 30-day Meta deletion periods above are VSP service commitments retained from the existing policy, not a statement that Meta grants a universal 30-day waiting period. Any shorter applicable legal or platform requirement takes precedence.

Deleting VSP social data does not remove videos already published on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube, or delete your accounts on those platforms. Remove that content on the relevant platform. Original VSP projects and media are separate; specify them if your request also concerns those files or your entire VSP account.
