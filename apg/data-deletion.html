User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Meta App: APG Social Publishing

Application: APG – AI Post Generator (tatdigital.com/ai-post-generator)

How to Delete Your Data

If you have connected your Facebook or Instagram account to APG and wish to delete all associated data (access tokens, account identifiers, scheduled posts), you can do so in two ways:

Option 1 – From within the app (immediate)

  1. Log in to APG at tatdigital.com/ai-post-generator
  2. Go to "Mis Publicaciones" → tab "Conexiones"
  3. Click "Desconectar" next to each connected account
  4. Your tokens and scheduled posts are deleted immediately from our servers

Option 2 – Email request

  1. Send an email to tatdigital@tatdigital.com
  2. Use subject line: "Data Deletion Request – APG"
  3. Include the email address associated with your APG account

We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days. A confirmation email will be sent once the process is complete.

What data is deleted

  • Facebook and Instagram access tokens (encrypted)
  • Facebook Page ID and Page name
  • Instagram Business Account ID and username
  • All scheduled and published post records associated with your account
