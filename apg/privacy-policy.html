Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026

Meta App: APG Social Publishing

Application: APG – AI Post Generator (tatdigital.com/ai-post-generator)

Operated by: TATDigital

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Facebook or Instagram account to APG, we access and store the following data through the Meta Graph API:

  • Your Facebook user ID and name
  • Your Facebook Page name, Page ID, and Page access token
  • Your Instagram Business Account ID and username
  • OAuth access tokens required to publish content on your behalf

We do not access your personal messages, contacts, friends list, or any data not explicitly listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Publish images and captions to your Instagram Business account
  • Publish images and captions to your Facebook Page
  • Schedule future publications on your behalf
  • Display the connection status within the APG application

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than those listed above.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in our database. They are never exposed to the browser or shared with third parties. All communication with Meta's Graph API occurs server-side over HTTPS.

4. Data Retention

We retain your connection data for as long as your APG account is active. You can delete your data at any time by disconnecting your account within the APG application or by submitting a deletion request (see Section 6).

5. Third-Party Services

APG uses the Meta Graph API (Facebook / Instagram) to publish content. Your use of these services is also subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of all your data at any time by:

  • Using the "Disconnect" button within APG (removes all stored tokens immediately)
  • Sending an email to tatdigital@tatdigital.com with subject "Data Deletion Request – APG"

We will confirm receipt within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions: tatdigital@tatdigital.com
